About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the Gran Sasso Science Institute, working with Dr. Amir Goharshady in the ALPACAS group on the ERC SPES project. My research interests are at the intersection of algorithms and program analysis, in particular, I study how structural and sparsity properties of programs and the SMT instances derived from them, can be turned into faster analysis and solver techniques.
